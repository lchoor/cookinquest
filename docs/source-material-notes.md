## What would help most (in priority order)

1. **Original artwork** — the chef mascot logo, the app icon, the India map graphic,
   the "on the Menu" card, the GAME STRATEGY seal. Anything vector (`.ai`, `.svg`,
   `.eps`) or high-res PNG beats a crop out of the composite.
2. **Real screen exports** — any original iPad screen PNGs, Photoshop/Illustrator
   files, or the InDesign/Keynote deck the Wix page was exported from.
3. **Process artifacts** — sketches, wireframes, flow diagrams, storyboards, the
   game-loop diagram, anything from before the final art. The old page has *zero*
   process content, so this is the single biggest gap in the story.
4. **Context notes** — a paragraph is plenty. Was this a course project, a personal
   project, a team project? What year? What was your role? What did you actually
   learn from it? Any feedback or playtesting you remember, even informally.
5. **Video** — any screen recording or walkthrough of the game.

Accepted formats: anything. PDFs, PSDs, AI, videos, screenshots, a Google Doc export,
a voice-memo transcript. I'll web-size whatever ships and leave the originals here.
