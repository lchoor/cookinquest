Phase 4

Cook

Prep intro Prep station: 5 appliances Doneness gauges: blue → green → red
Decision

Load the right ingredient onto the right appliance and pull it before the gauge overshoots red.

Economic effect

No new spend here, but every ingredient on the station is whichever tier got bought back in Phase 3.

Feeling

Hands on and a little tense: a race against a gauge, with the earlier shopping choices now baked in.

Phase 5

Judgement

Final plated dish Critic review: 1 to 5 stars
Decision

None: this is the payoff. The critic rates what was hunted, bought and cooked.

Suspense, then pride or disappointment: the one moment the whole run gets marked.

Designed now, not recovered

These five mechanics had no answer anywhere in the 2016 source. Kept separate from the map above so nothing on this page is mistaken for recovered history.

  1. 1

    Star thresholds and review copy

    No 1 to 4 star thresholds or review text existed in the source.

    New 25 point model: ingredients out of 12, cooking out of 10, time out of 3. 23 to 25 = 5 stars, 18 to 22 = 4, 12 to 17 = 3, 6 to 11 = 2, 0 to 5 = 1.

  2. 2

    Bonus formula

    No formula existed for how Speed or Time bonus was calculated.

    New: Speed = $1 per item found unaided, max $15. Time = $1 per 10 seconds remaining against a 180 second par, max $18.

  3. 3

    Wrong tap penalty

    No penalty amount existed for a mistaken tap during the hunt.

    New: minus $2, with a 1 second cooldown before the next tap registers.

  4. 4

    Six cuisines versus nine map slots

    The game copy promises six different cuisines, but the map shows nine restaurant slots.

    Resolved as 9 slots, 6 playable restaurants unlocking in sequence, 3 marked coming soon.

  5. 5

    Timed versus Relaxed effect

    The choice existed on screen but had no defined mechanical effect in the source.

    New: Timed runs a hunt countdown and pays both bonuses. Relaxed has no countdown and pays the Speed Bonus only.
