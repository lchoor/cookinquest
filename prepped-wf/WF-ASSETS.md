## Notes on method
- All source slides are 1920x1080 renders. Crop boxes above are tight to the actual drawn/typed content, excluding slide titles and empty margin except where noted (icon-rationale's title overlap; competitor slide's title is the whole point of that asset per the brief).
- Several wireframe assets came back under their requested target width after tight cropping — this is expected and not a defect: these are photographs of a physical notebook/whiteboard page held at a slight angle, so only ~1200-1300px of the 1920px frame is actually the drawn page content. Per the brief's method, none of these were upscaled; native size was kept and is noted above.
- Two assets required a second crop pass after the first review caught clipped content: `wf-plating.jpg` (was cutting off the final "n" of "Gulab Jamun") and `wf-khanatown.jpg` (was cutting the roof finial at top and the lamp-post at right). Both are now complete in the final files.
