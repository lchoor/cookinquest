3 · Complete payoff: cook, judgement, progress

Phase 4 · Cook

Group every collected ingredient, load the five labeled appliances, and use drag or tap-select/tap-place. Each station moves through Heating → Ready → Overcooking. All five must resolve before the chai and thaali assemble.

Phase 5 · Judgement

The critic scores Ingredient Quality /12 and Cooking /10. The total out of 22 creates 1–5 stars and a short review that explains what helped or hurt the result.

Phase 6 · Progress

The next restaurant visibly unlocks on the map, receives a fresh $100 budget, and becomes playable. Taj Mahal remains available to replay for a better rating.

4 · iPadOS 26 layer

Content first

Full-bleed scenes stay dominant. The course list collapses into a readable drawer when the iPad window narrows.

Glass only for controls

Pause, Help, and compact status controls can float in restrained Liquid Glass. Lists, prices, story, and gauges remain opaque.

Every input works

Touch, Pencil, pointer, keyboard, VoiceOver, and Switch Control can complete the same full journey. Dragging is never required.

States explain themselves

44×44 pt controls, text progress, checkmarked selections, labeled gauge zones, visible budget arithmetic, and no color-only meaning.

No-shortcut commitment: the Figma mockup links all 60 item finds, four completion summaries, four shopping ladders, four purchase confirmations, four hub-return states, five cooking stations, the score breakdown, and the restaurant unlock. Components and variables reduce authoring cost, but the player never skips a level.
